Your information, in plain words

Last updated October 30, 2026

Draft. This policy is a plain-language draft and is still being reviewed. It hasn’t been checked by a lawyer yet. If something here is unclear, email aew.workflow@gmail.com.

The short version. We collect what you give us: your name and email when you sign in, and the tasks and files you hand to your AI Employee. We use it to do the work and to run the service. We don’t sell your information, and we don’t show you ads. Email us any time and we’ll delete it.

Who we are

AI Employee Workflow (“AEW”, “we”) runs aiemployeeworkflow.com. You can reach us at aew.workflow@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your account. When you sign in with Google or LinkedIn, we get your name, your email address and an account ID from that provider. We never see your password. We also note when you first signed in and when you last did.
  • Your tasks and files. What you type or say to your AI Employee, any file you upload (PDF, Word, TXT, CSV or MD), your answers to its questions, and the finished work it sends back.
  • Sign-up forms. If you claim a founding spot or join the waiting list: your name, email, and anything else you choose to add, like what you do, your phone number and the first task you’d hand off.
  • Basic usage. To keep the service fair and safe we keep a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address to apply daily limits, and our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs. Your browser stores a random ID so your AI Employee can keep a conversation together. We may use Cloudflare’s privacy-friendly web analytics, which counts visits without cookies.
  • Cookies. We use one cookie to keep you signed in, and a short-lived one (about 10 minutes) that protects the sign-in step. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

If you talk instead of type, your browser turns your voice into text. Depending on your browser, that may be done by your browser’s maker (for example, Google for Chrome). We only receive the text.

Why we use it

  • To do the tasks you hand off and send the work back to you.
  • To keep your account and know it’s you.
  • To stop spam and abuse (daily limits and a bot check).
  • To reply to you, and to contact you about the founding program if you signed up for it.
  • To fix problems and improve the service.

Who helps us process it

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and stores accounts, tasks and sign-ups. It also runs Turnstile, the bot check on our forms, and delivers our sign-up emails.
  • The AI model provider, through OpenRouter. To do a task, your AI Employee sends the task, your answers and any file contents to an AI model through OpenRouter. They process it to produce the work.
  • Our own computer. Your AI Employee runs on a computer AEW operates. It picks up each task, works on it, and sends the result back.
  • Google or LinkedIn, only for signing you in.
  • Our team’s notifications. When a task or sign-up comes in, we get a short alert (through Telegram, Slack or email) so a real person can keep an eye on things. These alerts can include part of your task or sign-up details.

We share information with these providers only so they can do their job for us. We don’t sell it or rent it to anyone, and we don’t share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded files: deleted as soon as the task finishes, or when it stops with an error.
  • Tasks and finished work: deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Your account (name and email): kept until you ask us to delete it.
  • Sign-up forms: kept while the founding program and waiting list are running, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Team alerts may stay in our messages until we clear them. We delete them when you ask us to delete your data.

Deleting your information

Email aew.workflow@gmail.com from the email address you signed in or signed up with, and tell us what you’d like deleted (or just say “everything”). We’ll confirm, and delete it within 30 days. You can also ask us for a copy of what we have about you.

Keeping it safe

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your sign-in cookie can’t be read by scripts on the page, and each task can only be opened by the account that sent it. No system is perfect, so please don’t upload passwords, card numbers or other information you wouldn’t want in a document.

Children

AEW is made for adults running a business or doing their job. It isn’t meant for children under 16, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top. If it’s a big change, we’ll tell signed-in users.
